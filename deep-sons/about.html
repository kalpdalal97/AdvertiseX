About us

A counter, a table and a tape measure

Deep Sons is a tailoring house and cloth merchant. We keep suiting and shirting by the length, and we make wedding and festive wear to order — sherwanis, indo-western, kurta jacket sets and jodhpuris, for men and for the smallest member of the party.

We sell cloth

Wools, wool blends, poplins, dobbies and silks, chosen by hand and kept on the shelf where you can feel them.

We cut it ourselves

Every length is chalked and cut on our own table. Nothing is sub-contracted, which is why we can alter what we make.

We don't sell online

This site is a look book. There are no prices here because they depend on the cloth and the work — we talk about them at the counter.

Hours

Before you come

  • Wedding wear: allow three to four weeks and two fittings.
  • Suiting: allow two weeks; a basted trial is included.
  • Shirting: bring a shirt that fits you well if you have one.
  • Bringing your own cloth is welcome.

What happens in store

From the shelf to the mirror

  • We pull the lengths you shortlisted and a few you did not expect
  • Measurements are taken by hand and kept on file for next time
  • You choose collar, cuff, length, lining and buttons
  • We agree a date, and you come back for the trial

Prices depend entirely on the cloth and the work, so we talk about them at the counter rather than publishing them here.
